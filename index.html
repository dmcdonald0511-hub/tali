Tali Support

Deliver with Intelligence. Help with your courier assistant: the automatic mileage tracker for gig drivers. Tali was called Tally Mile until October 2026.

Most problems have a quick fix below. For anything else, email talicourieradmin@gmail.com. You'll usually hear back within a day or two.

Drives aren't recording

  • Open Settings → Tracking check-up in the app. It checks every phone setting Tali needs and has a Fix button for anything that's off.
  • Location must be set to "Always." iPhone Settings → Tali → Location → Always, with Precise Location on. "While Using" isn't enough, because drives are recorded while your phone is locked.
  • If you paused tracking, it turns back on by itself at the end of the pause, or you can tap Resume on the Drives tab.

A drive is missing, started late, or ended early

  • Add a missed drive with the + button on the Drives tab, or tap the microphone and say it, like "Drove from home to Walmart."
  • Tap any drive to change its miles or addresses.
  • Help us fix it: in the app, go to Settings → Tracking check-up → Send a report. It shows what the app saw and when (times and speeds, never where you went), and you choose who to send it to.

Riding in someone else's car

  • While a drive is recording, tap I'm a passenger on the Live drive screen or on your Lock Screen. That drive won't be saved, and nothing is recorded until the ride is over.
  • If you take the wheel partway through, tap I'm driving now on the Drives tab.
  • Already saved? Open the drive and tap I was a passenger to remove it.

Voice commands aren't working

  • Voice runs on your phone and needs Apple's speech download: iPhone Settings → General → Keyboard → turn on Enable Dictation, then wait a few minutes on Wi-Fi.
  • Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Tali in iPhone Settings → Tali.
  • Try: "Drove from home to Walmart for DoorDash," "My last drive was business," or "Add a stop at 410 Decatur Pike." You always check what it heard before anything saves.

Saved places

  • Open a drive and tap Save next to its From or To address to name that place, like Home or Walmart pickup.
  • Rename, move, or remove places in Settings → Saved places. Drives that start or end there show the name in your log and reports.

Plan Your Week and your goal

Plan Your Week, Car Costs, Hours & Breaks, electric cars, and Tali on iPad and Mac come with the next update after Tali 3.0.

  • With Tali Pro, open Plan Your Week from the goal ring on the Drives tab, or from ••• → Plan My Week. Set what you want to make in a week after car costs.
  • Tali suggests the times that have paid you best, from your own pay by day and time over the last 12 weeks. Rain in the forecast and holidays raise the estimate, and times with a severe weather warning aren't suggested.
  • Tap a block to add or remove it, get a reminder before each one, or add them to your calendar. Tali can only add events, so remove one you no longer plan in the Calendar app.
  • The ring on the Drives tab fills toward your goal. Tap it to switch between this week and this month. The monthly goal follows your weekly goal unless you set your own.

Car costs and profit

  • Settings → Car Costs shows what a mile costs you to drive: gas or charging, upkeep, and owning the car (your payment or depreciation, insurance and registration).
  • Gas comes from the price you type, your latest gas receipt, or this week's U.S. Energy Information Administration average for your area.
  • With Pro, each drive you enter pay for shows its profit after these costs. Your tax deduction doesn't change; it still uses the IRS rate.

Hours and breaks

  • Tali counts your hours on the clock from your work drives, free for everyone. It warns you 2 hours, 1 hour and 30 minutes before Uber and Lyft's 12-hour limit; the apps' own count is the final word.
  • Set your own daily and weekly limits, break reminders and days off in Settings → Hours & Breaks.

Electric cars

  • Turn on This car is electric in Settings → Car Costs. Home and public charging take the place of gas, Car Care leaves out oil changes, and the + button on the Drives tab has a Charging Stop.

Tali on iPad and Mac

  • Your iPhone records your drives. Tali on an iPad, or on a Mac with Apple silicon, shows your iPhone's latest backup so you can look through it, print, or export.
  • With the same Apple ID and iCloud Keychain on, it finds the backup by itself. Otherwise, enter the Recovery Code from your iPhone (Settings → Backup).
  • Sort and change drives on your iPhone; they show on the iPad or Mac after its next backup. An iPad that rides along in the car can record its own drives instead.

Offer codes

  • Got an App Store offer code? Go to Settings → Redeem a code → Redeem an App Store code. Apple's sheet adds the free time to Tali Pro.

Subscription and billing

  • Every feature is free for your first 30 days. After that, tracking with no drive limit, sorting, drive history, adding missed drives by hand, your year's mileage log as a spreadsheet, car care, Hours & Breaks, emergency help and backups stay free.
  • Tali Pro ($6.99 a month or $59.99 a year in the US) keeps route planning, Plan Your Week, profit on each drive, pay per mile, taxes to set aside, the missed-drive finder, PDF reports, receipts, voice, the Apple Watch app, group sharing and more.
  • New subscribers get a 1-month free trial through the App Store. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and you won't be charged.
  • Manage or cancel Tali Pro anytime in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • New phone? Tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. You won't be charged again.

Your data

Your drives, places, and receipts are stored on your phone. A backup copy is kept on Tali's server, locked with your own Recovery Code so only you can open it; turn it off or delete it in Settings → Backup. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what the app uses and why.
