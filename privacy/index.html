Tally Mile Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Tally Mile records your drives so you can claim every mile you're owed. Your drives, places, and receipts are kept on your phone, and the backup copy is locked with your own Recovery Code before it leaves, so no one else can read it, including us. There's no account, no ads, no analytics trackers, and nothing is ever sold.

What Tally Mile uses, and why

Location (GPS)Detects when a drive starts and ends, draws its route, and counts the miles without you doing anything.Stored on your phone. Sent to your own Apple Watch, if you have one, directly from phone to watch.

What Tally Mile does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, ad networks, or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking services.
  • Your locations, drives, places, and receipts are never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Where your data lives

Everything you record is kept in a database on your phone, the same way a notes app keeps your notes. The Home Screen widget and Lock Screen Live Activity read from it on the phone. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud, that backup can include this data under Apple's iCloud terms. Deleting Tally Mile deletes this data from your phone.

Your Recovery Code backup

  • On by default. Turn it off any time in Settings › Backup › Back Up with Recovery Code.
  • Locked on your phone. Everything is encrypted before it's sent, with a key made from your Recovery Code. Without the code, the backup can't be read, by us or anyone else.
  • Where the code is kept. On your phone, and in iCloud Keychain (on Android, your Google account), so a new phone signed in to the same account can find it. You can also save or print it. Keep it somewhere safe: anyone with the code can restore your records.
  • Deleting it. Settings › Backup › Delete Backup from Server removes the backup and every receipt photo in it from our server right away, and turns the backup off. Make a New Code backs up again under a new code and deletes the old backup.
  • How long it's kept. Until you delete it. The backup is tied only to your Recovery Code, not to your name or email, so we can't look it up for you. Delete it from the app before you remove Tally Mile if you don't want it kept.

Services we rely on

  • Apple: App Store purchases, Apple Maps lookups, and, if you use them, iCloud backups.
  • RevenueCat: checks and manages Tally Mile Pro subscriptions.
  • Supabase: our promo-code server, and where your Recovery Code backup is stored. It receives your records only after they're locked on your phone, and never your Recovery Code.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass): speed limits, road cautions, and building outlines on the live drive map.
  • FCC census lookup and NHTSA crash records: the county along your route and its fatal-crash history, for road cautions.

Children

Tally Mile is made for drivers tracking mileage for work and taxes. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If the way Tally Mile handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
