Tali Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Tali (formerly Tally Mile) records your work drives and helps you keep track of your pay and mileage. Your drives, places, pay, and receipts are kept on your phone, and the backup copy is locked with your own Recovery Code before it leaves, so no one else can read it, including us. Our server can read only what you share with a group (your name and latest location), records of founder's-deal purchases, and records of promo codes redeemed in earlier versions. There's no account, no ads, no analytics trackers, and we never sell or share your personal information.

Who we are

Tali is made by David McDonald, in the United States ("we", "us"). Tali is offered only in the United States, on the App Store and Google Play there, and this policy is written for U.S. users. Email talicourieradmin@gmail.com with any question about this policy.

Information about other people

Your work involves other people: customers, riders, the businesses you pick up from. Tali is built to keep as little about them as it can, and only on your phone or in your locked backup, which we can't read:

  • Addresses. By default, drives keep the street and city of a drop-off, not the house number. A route's stops are cleared 6 hours after the last one, or after a day left alone, and are never in a backup.
  • Notes and codes. A note left on a route stop stays on your phone only and is forgotten after 7 days. Codes and notes for buildings and businesses belong on saved places. Don't put customers' names, phone numbers, or other personal details in notes.
  • Screenshots. From an order or weekly pay screenshot, only the lines with amounts, distances, times, dates, pay words, and the app's name are kept; names, addresses, and phone numbers are dropped right in the Share menu. Nothing from an offer screenshot is kept.
  • Groups. While you're stopped near a route stop, your group sees a spot rounded to about a quarter mile, not the house.

We don't receive, sell, or share any of this. You decide what you enter, and you can delete it any time (Settings › Places, or Erase All My Data). If someone asks us about information you may have entered about them, we'll explain that it's kept only on your device and in your locked backup, which we can't open.

What Tali does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, ad networks, or advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No analytics or tracking services of our own. On Android, Google's built-in text recognition and Google Maps may send Google diagnostic information under Google's terms.
  • No readable history of where you've been on our server. Your drives go there only inside your locked backup, and a group sees only your latest location.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information, and we use your precise location only to provide the features you use.

Where your data lives

Everything you record is kept in a database on your phone, the same way a notes app keeps your notes. If your phone backs up to iCloud or Google, that backup can include this data under their terms. Earlier versions of the app saved backups to your own iCloud Drive; Tali can still restore those but no longer adds to them.

Deleting Tali deletes this data from your phone. Your phone keeps a few small items in its secure storage after an app is deleted, so they come back if you reinstall: when your free trial started, a promo code you redeemed, the install ID, and your Recovery Code.

Our server (Supabase) is in the United States. Like most web services, it and the map and weather services above keep standard request logs, such as your network address and the time, for a short time to run and protect the service.

Group location sharing

  • Only if you choose it. Nothing is shared until you start or join a group in Settings › Group and tap Start sharing or Join and share. Groups are for adults: you must confirm you’re 18 or older to start or join one. An invite code only asks to join; the group’s owner has to let you in. Until then you see no one, and only the name you entered is kept with your request so the owner can decide; no location is shared. Requests to join expire after 24 hours. When someone joins, everyone in the group is told their name.
  • What the group sees. The name you give, your latest location on a map, how long ago it was updated, and your speed while you're driving.
  • When it's sent. All the time, including when you're not driving and when drive tracking is paused: up to every 30 seconds on the road, and every few minutes otherwise. It stops when you turn off Share my location or leave the group.
  • Invite codes. Anyone with your group's invite code can join and see the group's locations, so share it only with people you trust. Codes stop working after 7 days.
  • What our server keeps. The group's name, each person's name, their latest location, when their app last checked in, and that they confirmed they’re 18 or older. Pending requests to join (a name, for up to 24 hours) and “joined” notices (a name, kept for 30 days) are stored too. Each new location replaces the last one, so there's no history. A member whose app hasn’t checked in for 30 days is removed automatically, locations older than 30 days are cleared, and empty groups are deleted.
  • Pausing and leaving. Turning off Share my location removes your location from the server. Leaving the group deletes your name and location, and a group is deleted when its last person leaves. The person who started a group can remove anyone from it.
  • Not for emergencies. No one watches the group map for emergencies. In an emergency, call 911.
  • Before you delete Tali. Leave the group first. If your Tali Pro ends, or you delete Tali without leaving, your last shared location stays visible to the group until you leave or are removed.

Your Recovery Code backup

  • On by default. Turn it off any time in Settings › Backup › Back Up with Recovery Code.
  • What's in it. Your drives and their edit history, saved places (with their codes and notes), vehicles, pay, receipts and receipt photos, emergency contacts, and settings.
  • Locked on your phone. Everything is encrypted before it's sent, with a key made from your Recovery Code. Without the code, the backup can't be read, by us or anyone else. Our server sees only locked files, their sizes, and when they were last updated.
  • Where the code is kept. On your phone, and in iCloud Keychain (on Android, your Google account's Block Store), so a new phone signed in to the same account can find it. You can also save, print, or share it. Anyone with the code can restore your records. We can't see, reset, or recover your code.
  • Deleting it. Settings › Backup › Delete Backup from Server removes the backup and every receipt photo in it from our server right away, and turns the backup off. Make a New Code backs up again under a new code and deletes the old backup.
  • How long it's kept. Until you delete it, or until it goes 12 months without being updated, whichever comes first. The backup is tied only to your Recovery Code, not to your name or email, so we can't look it up for you.

Your choices and rights

  • On your phone. You can see, change, export, and delete your drives and records in the app at any time. You can turn off the backup, the speed limit sign, road cautions, and group sharing in Settings.
  • On our server. You can ask to know, get a copy of, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you: a group membership, a founder's-deal record, or a promo-code record. Your locked backup can be deleted from the app. Email talicourieradmin@gmail.com. We'll answer within 45 days, and won't treat you differently for asking. If we turn down a request, you can ask us to reconsider by replying to our answer; if you're still not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general.
  • No sale or sharing. We don't sell or share personal information, including of anyone under 16, and don't use it for targeted advertising or profiling.
  • Sensitive data. Precise location is used only to provide the features you use, such as recording your drives and group sharing if you join one.
  • Do Not Track. Tali doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, so it treats browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals the same: there's nothing to opt out of.

Children

Tali is for drivers 18 and older who track mileage for work and taxes. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used a Tali group, email us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If the way Tali handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. If a change is significant, Tali will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Questions, or want something deleted?

Before you do, leave any group, and delete your Recovery Code backup from the app (Settings › Backup › Delete Backup from Server) if you don't want it kept. We'll help with the rest, including removing you from a group or deleting a record on our server.
