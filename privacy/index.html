Tali Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Tali (formerly Tally Mile) records your drives so you can claim every mile you're owed. Your drives, places, pay, and receipts are kept on your phone, and the backup copy is locked with your own Recovery Code before it leaves, so no one else can read it, including us. The only things our server can read are what you share with a group (your name and latest location) and records of promo codes redeemed in earlier versions. There's no account, no ads, no analytics trackers, and nothing is ever sold.

What Tali does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, ad networks, or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking services.
  • No readable history of where you've been on our server. Your drives go there only inside your locked backup, and a group sees only your latest location.
  • Your locations, drives, places, and receipts are never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Where your data lives

Everything you record is kept in a database on your phone, the same way a notes app keeps your notes. The Home Screen widget and Lock Screen Live Activity read from it on the phone. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud, that backup can include this data under Apple's iCloud terms. Earlier versions of the app saved backups to your own iCloud Drive instead; Tali can still restore those but no longer adds to them, and they stay in your iCloud until you remove them.

Deleting Tali deletes this data from your phone. iOS keeps a few small items in the iPhone's Keychain after an app is deleted, so they come back if you reinstall: when your free trial started, a promo code you redeemed, the install ID, and your Recovery Code.

Group location sharing

  • Only if you choose it. Nothing is shared until you start or join a group on the Group tab and tap Start sharing or Join and share.
  • What the group sees. The name you give, your latest location on a map, how long ago it was updated, and your speed while you're driving.
  • When it's sent. All the time, including when you're not driving and when drive tracking is paused: up to every 30 seconds on the road, and every few minutes otherwise. It stops when you turn off Share my location or leave the group.
  • What our server keeps. The group's name, each person's name, their latest location, and when their app last checked in. Each new location replaces the last one, so there's no history. Invite codes stop working after 7 days.
  • Pausing and leaving. Turning off Share my location removes your location from the server. Leaving the group deletes your name and location, and a group is deleted when its last person leaves. The person who started a group can remove anyone from it.
  • Before you delete Tali. Leave the group first. If your Tali Pro ends, or you delete Tali without leaving, your last shared location stays visible to the group until you leave or are removed.

Your Recovery Code backup

  • On by default. Turn it off any time in Settings › Backup › Back Up with Recovery Code.
  • What's in it. Your drives and their edit history, saved places, vehicles, pay, receipts and receipt photos, emergency contacts, and settings.
  • Locked on your phone. Everything is encrypted before it's sent, with a key made from your Recovery Code. Without the code, the backup can't be read, by us or anyone else. Our server sees only locked files, their sizes, and when they were last updated.
  • Where the code is kept. On your phone, and in iCloud Keychain (on Android, your Google account), so a new phone signed in to the same account can find it. You can also save, print, or share it. Keep it somewhere safe: anyone with the code can restore your records.
  • Deleting it. Settings › Backup › Delete Backup from Server removes the backup and every receipt photo in it from our server right away, and turns the backup off. Make a New Code backs up again under a new code and deletes the old backup.
  • How long it's kept. Until you delete it, or until it goes 12 months without being updated, whichever comes first. While backups are on, Tali updates it every day you drive or open the app, so it's only deleted after your phone has gone unused, or had backups turned off, for a year. When backups are off, Settings › Backup shows the date it will be deleted. The backup is tied only to your Recovery Code, not to your name or email, so we can't look it up for you. Delete it from the app before you remove Tali if you don't want it kept until then.

Services we rely on

  • Apple: App Store purchases, Apple Maps lookups, iCloud Keychain for your Recovery Code, and, if you use them, iCloud backups and Siri. When you use a Tali shortcut with Siri, Siri handles what you say under Apple's terms.
  • RevenueCat: checks and manages Tali Pro purchases.
  • Supabase: our server. It holds group locations while you share them, promo-code records, and your Recovery Code backup. It receives your records only after they're locked on your phone, and never your Recovery Code.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass): speed limits, road cautions, and building outlines on the live drive map.
  • FCC census lookup and NHTSA crash records: the county along your route and its fatal-crash history, for road cautions.

Children

Tali is made for drivers tracking mileage for work and taxes. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If the way Tali handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
