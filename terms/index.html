  • Your safety and the law come first. Don't type, tap, read, or look at Tali, your Apple Watch, or your phone while the car is moving. Pull over, or use your voice where it's legal and safe.

  • Directions, speed limits, road cautions, crash history, and building outlines come from map data (including OpenStreetMap, a volunteer project) that can be missing, wrong, or out of date.   • Weather alerts come from the National Weather Service when Tali can check, and can be missing, late, or wrong. They aren't an emergency alert system: keep your phone's Emergency Alerts on and follow local authorities.

While the car is moving, Tali doesn't read offer screenshots; it only reminds you to check offers when you're stopped.

  • Automatic tracking depends on your phone's location, motion, and battery settings, and can miss or cut short a drive. Look over your drives regularly, and keep your gig apps' own records to back them up. You're responsible for the accuracy of your mileage log and anything you file.
  • You're responsible for what you save about places, such as gate codes and notes, and for following your gig apps' rules about customer information. Tali keeps each drive's start and end for your mileage log, by default as street and city without the house number (you can change this in Settings), keeps notes left on route stops on your phone only and for 7 days, clears a finished route's stops 6 hours after the last one, and keeps only the amounts, distances, times, and dates it reads from a shared screenshot (nothing at all from an offer screenshot). Don't put customers' names, phone numbers, or other personal information in notes, and don't share screenshots that show them.
  • The document vault keeps a convenience copy, not an official document or proof. A photo of a driver's license isn't a license. Keep the originals; documents in the vault aren't backed up.
  • Your records are kept on your device. Keep your backup on and your Recovery Code safe. We can't see, reset, or recover your Recovery Code, so if you lose both your phone and the code, your backup can't be opened.
  • Answers from Tali, the voice assistant, are worked out from your own records and can be wrong or misheard. Check anything important on the screen.
